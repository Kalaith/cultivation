⚡ Tribulation System (Heavenly Judgment)

A mandatory boss encounter triggered when advancing to major realms. It is not just a health check, but a test of preparation, karma, and Dao.

### 1. Tribulation Mechanics

**Structure:**
- **Waves**: Tribulations come in waves of lightning strikes.
- **Intermission**: Short breathing room between waves to pop pills or repair arrays.
- **Heart Demon Phase**: The final wave is often internal, attacking the Spirit/Mind rather than Body.

**Lightning Types:**
| Type | Color | Effect | Counter |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Pure Lightning** | Blue | Raw HP damage breakdown. | High Defense / Body Cultivation |
| **Soul Lightning** | Purple | Bypasses armor, hits Spirit/Mind. | Soul Artifacts / Willpower |
| **Karma Lightning** | Red | Damage scales with "Sin" or "Killing Intent". | Merit / Cleansing Rituals |
| **Void Lightning** | Black | Erases existence (Insta-kill chance if low HP). | Space Dao / Void Anchors |
| **Heart Lightning** | White | Hallucinations; controls reverse, friendly fire. | Mind Calmness / Heart Demon resistance |

---

### 2. Realm-Specific Tribulation Profiles

#### **Golden Core Tribulation (Azoth Thunder)**
*The separation of mortal and cultivator. The heavens test if your body can hold the Dao.*
- **Waves**: 3 (Mortal), 6 (Earth), 9 (Heavenly)
- **Primary Damage**: Pure Lightning (Physical)
- **Mechanic**: "Shell Cracking" - Each strike reduces max defense.
- **Failure**: Cultivation destroyed, back to Mortal.

#### **Nascent Soul Tribulation (Yin-Yang Fire)**
*The birth of the spiritual self. Lightning turns into spectral fire.*
- **Waves**: 9
- **Primary Damage**: Soul Lightning + Heat (DoT)
- **Mechanic**: "Body Shedding" - Physical body takes massive damage; player must detatch Nascent Soul to survive the final strike, risking exposure.
- **Failure**: Body destroyed, must switch to Ghost Cultivation.

#### **Spirit Severing Tribulation (Karma Severing)**
*The heavens settle the score. Past deeds manifest as attacks.*
- **Waves**: 18
- **Primary Damage**: Karma Lightning
- **Mechanic**: "Echoes" - Enemies you killed in the game spawn as lightning constructs to attack you.
- **Failure**: Death (Soul dissipation).

#### **Ascension Tribulation (Nine Heavens Void)**
*The world tries to keep you. Gravity and space break down.*
- **Waves**: 36 to 81
- **Primary Damage**: Void Lightning
- **Mechanic**: "Reality Tear" - Safety tiles disappear; you must fly/hover and dodge spatial rifts while tanking hits.
- **Failure**: Exiled to the Void (Lost in space/time).

#### **Dao Ancestor Tribulation (Origin Silence)**
*The Dao tests its rival. Silence falls.*
- **Waves**: 1 (Infinite duration)
- **Primary Damage**: Concept Erasure
- **Mechanic**: "Re-writing" - The game UI glitches, controls change, stats vanish. You must use your "Dao Authority" to rebuild your existence in real-time.
- **Failure**: Save file deletion (or "Reincarnation" NG+).

---

### 3. Preparation & Intervention (Sect Gameplay)

You don’t have to face it alone. But help comes at a price.

**A. Formations (Arrays)**
- **Lightning Rod Array**: Redirects 30% damage to the mana pool.
- **Spirit Gathering Array**: Increases Qi regen between waves.
- **Turtle Shell Array**: Blocks one fatal hit, then shatters.

**B. Assistance (Sect Members)**
- **Elders**: Can step in to tank hits.
    - *Risk*: Tribulation difficulty scales up by 50% for each helper.
    - *Risk*: Elders can die permanently.
- **Disciples**: Can chant sutras to buff you.
    - *Risk*: Shockwaves can kill weak disciples nearby.

**C. Artifacts**
- **Substitution Puppet**: Takes death in your place (Consumable).
- **Karma Umbrella**: Reduces Karma Lightning damage.
- **Grounding Boots**: Pass pure lightning into the terrain (damages map).

### 4. Aftermath

**Success Rewards:**
- **Realm Advancement**: Access to new tier.
- **Tribulation Essence**: Crafting material for God-tier artifacts.
- **Dao Mark**: Permanent passive buff based on how you tanked it (e.g., "Thunder Body" if you tanked with face).

**Failure Penalties:**
- **Death**: Game Over (Roguelike mode).
- **Loose Cultivator**: Survive but sect/resources destroyed.
- **Dao Injury**: Permanent stat cap reduction until healed by rare item.
