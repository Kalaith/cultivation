# Wuxia/Cultivation Game Systems - Design Reference

**Focus: Quantity over Quality** - Pick and combine what interests you!

---

## COMBAT SYSTEMS

### Basic Combat Mechanics
1. **Turn-Based Combat** - Classic grid/non-grid, takes actions per turn
2. **Real-Time Action Combat** - Action points or stamina drain per move
3. **Stance-Based Combat** - Switch between offensive/defensive stances
4. **Qi Flow Combat** - Manage internal energy during attacks
5. **Five Elements Triangle** - Wood > Metal > Earth > Water > Fire > Wood
6. **Elemental Weakness System** - Fire beats Wood, Wood beats Earth, etc.
7. **Position-Based Combat** - Front/middle/back ranks with different bonuses
8. **Combo System** - Chain attacks for increased damage
9. **Counter-Attack Mechanics** - Parry and riposte system
10. **Range-Based (Melee/Ranged/Magic)** - Distance determines available actions

### Advanced Combat Features
11. **Chi/Qi Accumulation** - Build internal energy to unleash powerful attacks
12. **Cooldown System** - Powerful moves require waiting periods
13. **Spirit Power Meter** - Generate special power that unlocks ultimate abilities
14. **Harmony/Balance System** - Maintain Yin-Yang balance for bonuses
15. **Momentum System** - Consecutive successful actions increase power
16. **Broken Stance System** - Enemies can be knocked into vulnerable state
17. **Critical Strike Chance** - Based on precision stat
18. **Lifesteal Mechanic** - Drain enemy HP with attacks
19. **Reflect/Counter Damage** - Return portion of damage taken
20. **Bleed/Poison/Burn Status** - Damage over time effects
21. **Charm/Confusion Status** - Affect enemy targeting/actions
22. **Paralysis/Stun Status** - Skip enemy turns
23. **Vulnerability Stack** - Lower defenses with each debuff
24. **Enemy Behavior Patterns** - Predict and counter specific moves
25. **Fear/Intimidation System** - Lower enemy accuracy or damage

### Environmental Combat
26. **Environmental Hazards** - Lava, spikes, moving platforms affect battle
27. **Weather Effects** - Rain increases water damage, sunshine increases fire
28. **Terrain Bonuses** - High ground advantage, water terrain bonus for certain arts
29. **Destructible Objects** - Break pillars, barrels for tactical advantage
30. **Trap System** - Hidden traps that trigger in combat

## SPIRIT BEAST / COMPANION SYSTEMS

### Beast Types & Acquisition
1. **Elemental Beasts** - Fire, Water, Wood, Metal, Earth aligned
2. **Mythical Beasts** - Phoenix, Dragon, Qilin, Turtle, Crane
3. **Monster Taming** - Catch wild beasts and recruit them
4. **Beast Egg Hatching** - Incubate and hatch eggs for growth
5. **Beast Fusion** - Combine two beasts into a stronger hybrid
6. **Beast Evolution** - Transform beasts through stages (juvenile > adult > ancient)
7. **Contract System** - Sign contracts with powerful beasts (limited uses)
8. **Beast Summoning** - Summon beasts temporarily in battle
9. **Permanent Companion** - Single beast that levels with you
10. **Beast Party** - Team of 3-6 beasts you can swap between

### Beast Mechanics
11. **Beast Stats** - Separate HP, ATK, DEF, SPD from player
12. **Beast Skills** - Each beast learns unique moves
13. **Beast Personality** - Affects loyalty and actions
14. **Affection/Bond Level** - Relationship affects combat performance
15. **Beast Hunger System** - Feed beasts or they weaken
16. **Beast Breeding** - Combine male/female beasts to create offspring
17. **Bloodline Purity** - Pure bloodlines are stronger
18. **Beast Armor/Equipment** - Equip beasts with gear
19. **Beast Passive Abilities** - Always-active stat boosts or effects
20. **Beast Combo Attacks** - Player + Beast team-up moves
21. **Beast Resurrection** - Revive beast in battle (limited uses)
22. **Beast Friendship Evolution** - Evolution tied to bond level, not items
23. **Genetic Inheritance** - Offspring inherit parent moves
24. **Beast Age System** - Older beasts are stronger but slower
25. **Training Montages** - Automatic XP gain when not in party

## CRAFTING SYSTEMS

### Material-Based Crafting
1. **Ingredient Gathering** - Hunt beasts, farm herbs, mine ore
2. **Recipe Discovery** - Find recipes in dungeons, from NPCs
3. **Recipe Difficulty** - Recipes require certain skill levels
4. **Material Quality Tiers** - Common, Uncommon, Rare, Legendary materials
5. **Material Purity** - Mixing wrong elements creates flawed items
6. **Bulk Crafting** - Make multiple items at once
7. **Overcraft System** - Spend extra resources for guaranteed quality
8. **Failure Chance** - Crafting can fail, lose materials
9. **Critical Success** - Random chance for +1 quality tier
10. **Material Synergy** - Certain combinations provide bonuses

### Item-Specific Crafting
11. **Weapon Crafting** - Create/upgrade swords, spears, whips, etc.
12. **Armor Crafting** - Forge robes, chest plates, accessories
13. **Pill/Elixir Creation** - Brew cultivation pills with effects
14. **Talisman Inscription** - Inscribe magical talismans on paper/fabric
15. **Potion Brewing** - Mix health/mana potions
16. **Poison Crafting** - Create toxins to poison enemies
17. **Scroll Creation** - Write scrolls with stored spells
18. **Artifact Forging** - Create legendary items (expensive, time-consuming)
19. **Jewelry Crafting** - Make rings, amulets, earrings with special effects
20. **Food Cooking** - Prepare dishes that give temporary buffs

### Advanced Crafting
21. **Enchanting System** - Add magical properties to crafted items
22. **Gem Socketing** - Insert gems into equipment for bonuses
23. **Item Reforging** - Rebuild item completely with new stats
24. **Repair System** - Fix broken equipment
25. **Disenchanting** - Break down items into components
26. **Legendary Crafting Quest** - Multi-step quest to craft one powerful item
27. **Alchemical Reactions** - Mix ingredients in specific orders for different results
28. **Kiln/Furnace Upgrade** - Improve crafting station to unlock better recipes
29. **Bulk Material Conversion** - Convert 10 common materials → 1 rare material
30. **Residue Collection** - Gather leftover materials from failed crafts

12. **Beast Master** - Specialized in commanding beasts

14. **Staff User** - Magic focus, crowd control

23. **Level-Based Abilities** - Unlock job skills at job level milestones
24. **Ability Inheritance** - Keep abilities when switching jobs
25. **Job-Exclusive Equipment** - Some gear only usable by specific jobs
26. **Job Quest Chain** - Unlock new job through quest line

## CULTIVATION PROGRESSION SYSTEMS

### Realm/Stage Progression
1. **Linear Realm System** - Qi Condensation > Foundation > Core > Tribulation > Immortal
2. **Realm Breakthrough Quest** - Boss fight or challenge to advance realms
3. **Bottleneck System** - Get stuck at realm caps temporarily
4. **Dual Cultivation** - Pair with partner to progress together (faster or special benefits)
5. **Heavenly Tribulation** - Survive lightning strikes to advance
6. **Comprehension Meditation** - Sit and meditate to gain realm experience
7. **Experience/Points System** - Earn cultivation points from activities
8. **Stage Requirements** - Must reach stat thresholds before advancing
9. **Multiple Realm Paths** - Choose different cultivation methods (righteous vs demonic)
10. **Realm-Specific Abilities** - Each realm unlocks unique techniques

### Advancement Methods
11. **Meditation/Passive Cultivation** - Automatic XP gain over time
12. **Combat Grinding** - Defeat enemies to gain XP
13. **Quest Completion XP** - Story and side quests give XP
14. **Pill Consumption** - Swallow elixirs to instantly gain experience
15. **Resource Nodes** - Absorb power from special locations (caves, mountains)
16. **Teacher Training** - NPCs teach you techniques for XP
17. **Enlightenment Events** - Random breakthrough moments
18. **Dharma Inheritance** - Absorb techniques from defeated masters
19. **Ancient Tomb Exploration** - Find and absorb sealed techniques
20. **Time Skip Periods** - In-game years pass automatically (narrative mechanic)

### Stat Systems
21. **Strength/Constitution/Speed/Spirit/Mind** - Five core stats
22. **Elemental Affinities** - Fire/Water/Wood/Metal/Earth percentages
23. **Talent/Root Qualification** - Inherent quality affects growth
24. **Karma/Heavenly Will** - Alignment system affecting progression
25. **Lifespan Stat** - Track years remaining before natural death
26. **Soul Strength** - Separate stat for soul-based powers
27. **Luck Stat** - Affects critical hits, material drops, breakthrough success

## IMPLEMENTATION SUGGESTION

**For your game, I'd recommend:**
- Pick 2-3 combat systems (e.g., Turn-Based + Stance System + Qi Accumulation)
- Choose 1-2 Companion mechanics (e.g., Elemental Beasts + Beast Evolution)
- Select 1-2 Crafting systems (e.g., Pill Brewing + Weapon Forging)
- Implement 2 Job Types minimum (e.g., Martial Artist + Mage)
- Add 1 Realm Progression system (e.g., Linear Realm with Breakthrough)
- Include 2-3 Skill Acquisition types (e.g., Leveling Tree + Books + Master Training)
- Implement all Core Stats (5 minimum)
- Add 1-2 Economy systems (basic shop + faction points)
- Pick 3+ Factions/Sects for world building
- Include Quality of Life features (saves, hotkeys, etc.)

Start with this core, then layer on additional systems as needed!
